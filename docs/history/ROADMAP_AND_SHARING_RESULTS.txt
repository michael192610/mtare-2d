WHAT CHANGED COMPARED WITH THE EARLIER 50 ms VERSION

The earlier run 20261111_162825_902782_FULL_COMMS_3_ROBOTS finished at 99.92% in
429 cycles. Its 0-2 pair was within 4 m in 231/424 both-moving cycles and in
the same cell in 124. The new full-comms run measured 70/706 and 15 respectively.
This run showed less side-by-side movement for that pair, but required more
cycles/travel. The simultaneous cost and knowledge changes and time-limited
search variation prevent attributing this difference to one component.
No speedup or general overlap reduction is established from a single run.

WHY PRIVATE MAPS MATTER

Previously robots automatically copied detailed occupancy. Now they exchange
global region states and connectivity, matching the intended M-TARE abstraction.
A robot may know a remote global route without possessing the detailed path.
It must observe local free space while navigating there. This changes decisions
and can add exploration/travel despite a more faithful global protocol.

REMAINING LIMITS

Local coverage evidence still uses abstract 2D frontier samples and adapted
counts. Direct roadmap links use known-free 2D paths instead of keypose graphs.
Cycles are synchronized; return-home and communication-seeking are excluded.
Next research comparisons need repeated seeds/scenes and matched budgets.
Final belief completion is deliberately separate from measured coverage.
